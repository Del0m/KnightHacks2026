**Question:** My legs are really sore two days after my first squat session. Is that normal?

**Answer:** Soreness a day or two after a new squat routine is common and usually fades within a few days. Light cycling or an easy walk can help it settle, and gradual build‑up with good form is a typical approach people use to keep knee tracking straight during squats and lunges.

**Question:** How deep should I go on a bodyweight squat?

**Answer:** A common cue is to lower until the thighs reach about parallel to the floor, keeping the chest up and knees aligned with the toes. This depth works well for many people and engages the quadriceps, glutes, and hamstrings.

**Question:** How can I feel my glutes more during lunges?

**Question:** No pain, just tight hamstrings after lunges. What helps them loosen up?

### pushup_easier (programming, expected llm, got ai)

**Question:** I can only do 3 push-ups. How do I build up?

**Answer:** One option is to place the hands on a bench, which makes the push‑up easier while the body stays in a straight line. ### press_stiff (soreness, expected llm, got ai)

**Question:** My shoulders feel stiff the morning after overhead presses. Any easier variation?

**Answer:** A lighter load with more repetitions often feels gentler on the shoulders. Switching to a neutral‑grip bar or using dumbbells can change the angle of the press. Some people also find an incline or decline version easier on the joint.
