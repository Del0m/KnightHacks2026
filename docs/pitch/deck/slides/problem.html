The problem

Two kinds of people train alone,
unsure if they're doing it right.

New to the gym

64%

don't know how heavy their weights should be.

44–48%

are too embarrassed to ask for help.

Coming back from injury

30–50%

don't do their home exercises as prescribed.

#1

reason people stop sport after ACL surgery: fear of reinjury.

Sources: PureGym gym-fear report (poll, methods undisclosed); Argent et al., JMIR mHealth 2018; Ardern et al., BJSM 2011

Both groups have the same need: someone checking their form, without the embarrassment of asking. Beginners don't know what weight or form is right; people coming back from injury are afraid of getting hurt again. The beginner figures come from a gym-chain poll with undisclosed methods, so say "a PureGym survey found", not "studies show".
