How it works

Five parts, each doing the job it's fastest at.

1 · Your phone

Camera, pose AI, form engine, silent screen.

YOLOv8n-pose on the Neural Engine, 17 joints per frame.

Too slow, or Android? Frames go to the cloud, with consent.

2 · Spotter API

Node + Express. Sessions, plans, feed, rehab. Relays cloud pose.

3 · Neon Postgres

Accounts, social, pgvector, rehab limits.

4 · Databricks

Bronze, silver, gold. MLflow form model scores each set. Syncs trends to Neon.

5 · Hugging Face

Cloud pose endpoint for slower phones. gpt-oss notes and plans, bge-small retrieval, Llama Guard. Hub hosts our models.
