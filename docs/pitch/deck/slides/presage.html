MLH · Best use of Presage

Your rest timer reads your breathing, not the clock.

During rest: glance at the phone and Presage SmartSpectra reads pulse and breathing rate from your face, with no wearable.

Ready check: "Breathing has settled, start when you're ready", or a little more rest if it hasn't.

Over weeks: heart-rate recovery between sets, next to form and range of motion.

Same privacy line: vitals are computed on the phone; only the numbers sync.

Show to MLH judges if we build it. Why it fits: Presage is real-time human sensing from a camera, and Spotter is already a camera-first coach. The pose camera sits 2–3 m away facing the body, so vitals are read during rest, when the lifter looks at the phone. Wording stays non-medical: "breathing has settled", never a diagnosis.
