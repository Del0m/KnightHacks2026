Safety and privacy

A fitness app that knows where its lines are.

Safety

No diagnosis or treatment advice. Never changes a physio's limits. Any mention of pain routes to "please check with your physio or a doctor".

Privacy

Video stays on the phone unless you opt into cloud pose; those frames are processed in memory and never stored. Deleting your account clears Neon and Databricks.

Keep this short unless a judge asks; the FAQ has the longer answers.
