Spotter

A quiet coach for new gym-goers and people coming back from injury, on the phone you already own.

Pose AI runs on the phone when it can, and in the cloud only when it can't.

How it fits together

Three speeds

During a rep · under 0.1 s
Phone, or cloud pose
Reps and form are checked on the phone's chip, or by our cloud pose endpoint when the phone is too slow. Feedback is silent and visual.
During rest · about 1.5 s
Phone → Databricks → Hugging Face
Our model scores the set and writes one kind rest note. If the cloud is slow, the phone's rules set the medal.
Over weeks
Plans and progress
New: a balanced weekly routine that works every muscle group.
Recovering: range of motion per joint, and a physio report.

What each sponsor's tech does

Hugging Face
Open models end to end: our pose and form models on the Hub, gpt-oss via Inference Providers, bge-small retrieval, Llama Guard, and a pose Inference Endpoint that offloads phones too slow to run it.
Databricks
Every set flows through bronze, silver and gold. An MLflow model on Model Serving scores sets during rest. Gold tables sync to Neon, so the app never waits on a warehouse.
Neon
System of record: Neon Auth, the social graph, pgvector for retrieval and buddy matching, rehab limits and Databricks insights. Dev branches plus a seeded demo branch.
Safety lines we hold: no diagnosis, no treatment advice, never changes a physio's limits, and any mention of pain routes to "please check with your physio or a doctor". Cloud pose needs consent; frames are processed in memory and never stored. Deleting an account clears it from Neon and Databricks.
