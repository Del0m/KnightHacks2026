SteadyForum pitch opening

Slide 1: SteadyForum and the requested description.
Slide 2: 1080p/60fps rendered entrance from an existing iPhone 15 Pro mesh. Eased Bezier translation and Euler rotation on all three axes, finishing with a 10 degree upward pitch.
Slide 3: Matching phone pose straightens and zooms into the supplied workout figure; phone hardware and app controls leave the frame.

The embedded H.264 clips start on slide entry and hold their final frame. Advance from slide 1 to 2 and 2 to 3 once each.
Title text remains editable; 3D motion is embedded as video. Blender animation scene files are in this directory.
