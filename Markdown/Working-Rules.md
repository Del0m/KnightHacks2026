# Working rules

- Commit locally. Do not push branches or publish/update pull requests unless explicitly requested again. This supersedes the earlier push workflow.
- Preserve teammates' work. Bring merge conflicts to the owner before resolving them.
- Put documentation in `Markdown/`.
- Keep the app usable without login: Train, Plan, and workouts are public; Me (You), Feed, and Buddies require a confirmed account.
- Keep the white/light-blue glass UI, firm typography, and smooth interactions.
- Do not create or use generative AI artwork. Small logos are permitted.
- Do not add automated coauthor/collaborator attribution.
