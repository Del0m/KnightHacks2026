## Fonts

Barlow Condensed Bold and Barlow Regular, SemiBold, and Bold are bundled locally for consistent rendering on web and native. Their license texts are in `Font-Licenses/`.

## Motion

The hero copy enters gently, the photograph settles from a small scale increase, and the guide button responds to press. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects and animated scrolling.
